AICBC-2026

Welcome to the 49th All India Cell Biology Conference and Symposium, the distinguished annual gathering of the Indian Society of Cell Biology (ISCB). This year the event is proudly co-hosted by the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Pune and the National Centre for Cell Science (NCCS), Pune, from the 9th to the 11th of December 2026.

With a theme centred on "Architecture of Life," the conference explores how living systems are organised across space and time — from molecules and biomolecular condensates to organelles, cells, tissues, and the dynamics of development and ageing. It asks a single question across every scale: what design principles keep a living system organised, robust, and alive?

The programme spans phase separation and membrane-less compartments, genome and chromatin architecture, proteostasis and cellular stress, the cell cycle and signalling dynamics, ageing and lifespan, and development and morphogenesis. With this comprehensive scope, the event addresses both foundational concepts and cutting-edge advances, fostering innovation and multidisciplinary dialogue.

In addition to enriching discussions, the conference features dedicated sessions for students and generous time slots for poster presentations, ensuring active participation and engagement. This annual meeting promises to serve as a vibrant platform for scientists, academicians, students, and innovators to share discoveries, exchange ideas, and deepen their knowledge. We eagerly look forward to welcoming you to Pune.

About the Indian Society of Cell Biology (ISCB)

The ISCB, formally established in 1976, is one of the leading scientific societies in India, dedicated to promoting research and education in cell biology. It fosters collaboration among researchers, organises conferences, hands-on workshops, and training programmes, and facilitates the dissemination of knowledge on cellular processes.

ISCB actively supports young scientists and advances the understanding of cell biology to drive progress in health, agriculture, and biotechnology. For more information, visit the ISCB website. To become a member, please check the membership details here.

About IISER Pune

The Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune, established in 2006, is a premier institution dedicated to integrating research and education in the basic sciences. It is recognised as an Institution of National Importance and is among India's foremost centres for biology, chemistry, physics, and mathematics.

IISER Pune hosts a vibrant cell and molecular biology community working across genome organisation, biophysics, microbiology, developmental biology, and ageing. Its world-class imaging and microscopy facilities, collaborative culture, and beautiful Pashan campus make it an ideal setting to explore the architecture of living systems.

About NCCS Pune

The National Centre for Cell Science (NCCS), established in 1986, is an autonomous institute of the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India, and one of the country’s principal centres for research in cell biology. It is located on the Savitribai Phule Pune University campus.

NCCS hosts the National Cell Repository, among India’s largest collections of authenticated cell lines, and its research spans chromatin biology and gene regulation, immunology and infection, cancer biology, stem cells and regenerative medicine, and microbial diversity. Together with IISER Pune, NCCS makes Pune one of the strongest concentrations of cell biology research in the country — a fitting setting for a meeting on the architecture of living systems.

How to Reach IISER Pune

By Air
Pune is well connected by air through Pune International Airport (PNQ), located approximately 17 km from the IISER Pune campus (~45 minutes by road, depending on traffic). The airport receives direct flights from major Indian cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata. App-based taxi services such as Uber, Ola, and Rapido are readily available at the airport for convenient travel to the campus.

By Train
Pune Junction Railway Station (PUNE) is well-connected to major cities across India through frequent express and superfast trains. IISER Pune is located about 9 km from Pune Junction (~30 minutes by road). App-based taxi services such as Uber, Ola, and Rapido are readily available at the station. The Pune Metro (Purple Line) also connects the city centre to the western suburbs near IISER Pune.

By Road
Pune is well connected by several major national highways and expressways. The Mumbai–Pune Expressway links Pune to Mumbai in approximately 3 hours. Long-distance buses operate regularly from cities such as Mumbai, Nashik, Aurangabad, Hyderabad, and Bengaluru, both through MSRTC and private operators. The campus is easily accessible from anywhere in Pune via the Pashan Road.

Weather Advisory for December in Pune

Pune experiences pleasant winter weather in December. Temperatures generally range from a high of approximately 28 °C during the day to a low of around 12 °C at night. Mornings and evenings can feel cool, especially on the IISER Pune campus (Pashan area, slightly higher elevation). We recommend bringing a light jacket or sweater for comfortable wear during early-morning and evening sessions. Days are typically sunny and dry — ideal for poster sessions and networking outside the auditorium.
