AICBC-2026

The 49th All India Cell Biology Conference and Symposium is the annual meeting of the Indian Society of Cell Biology, co-hosted this year by IISER Pune and BRIC-NCCS Pune, 9–12 December 2026.

With a theme centred on “Architecture of Life”, the conference explores how cellular life is organised across space and time — from molecules and biomolecular condensates to organelles, cells, tissues, and the dynamics of development and ageing. It asks a single question across every scale: what design principles keep a living system organised, robust, and alive?

The programme is organised around broad areas of cell biology — Organelles and Trafficking, Host–Pathogen Interactions, The Nucleus, The Cytoskeleton, and Lipids and Metabolism — together with named endowment lectures, special seminars, and short student talks. It opens with pre-conference workshops at both host institutes on 9 December and closes at BRIC-NCCS Pune on 12 December.

About the Indian Society of Cell Biology (ISCB)

Founded in 1976, the ISCB promotes research and education in cell biology across India — running conferences, workshops and training programmes, and supporting young scientists. Membership is open to researchers and students: see iscb.co.in.

Endowment awards and travel grants are open to ISCB members only. If you intend to be considered for either, join the Society before you submit your abstract.

About IISER Pune

The Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune, established in 2006, integrates research and education in the basic sciences and is recognised as an Institution of National Importance.

Its cell and molecular biology community works across genome organisation, biophysics, microbiology, development and ageing, supported by extensive imaging and microscopy facilities on the Pashan campus.

About BRIC-NCCS Pune

The BRIC-National Centre for Cell Science (BRIC-NCCS), established in 1986, is an institute of the Department of Biotechnology’s Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council (BRIC), Government of India, and one of the country’s principal centres for research in cell biology. It is located on the Savitribai Phule Pune University campus.

It hosts the National Cell Repository, among India’s largest collections of authenticated cell lines, and its research spans chromatin biology, immunology and infection, cancer biology, stem cells and regenerative medicine, and microbial diversity.

Conference Venues

The meeting runs across two campuses. The first three days are held at IISER Pune, with all talks in Raman Hall, Lecture Hall Complex, and poster sessions and meals in the Dining Hall Complex. The final day (12 December) moves to NCCS Pune for Sessions 5 and 6, the endowment lecture and the award ceremony.

Pre-conference workshops run on 9 December at both IISER Pune and NCCS Pune. Places are limited and require separate sign-up during registration.

Accommodation

Campus accommodation is optional. If you take it, it is bundled into your fee and allocated on the IISER Pune campus — students in shared hostel rooms, post-docs in shared guest-house rooms, faculty, industry and international delegates in single rooms. No separate booking is needed.

Making your own arrangements? Register at the lower rate for your category.

Getting between the campuses

A shuttle will be provided to transfer delegates from IISER Pune to BRIC-NCCS Pune on the morning of 12 December.

The organisers do not arrange transport to or from the airport, railway station or bus terminals. App-based taxis (Uber, Ola, Rapido) are readily available at all of these and reach the IISER Pune campus in about 30–45 minutes.

How to Reach IISER Pune

By Air
Pune is well connected by air through Pune International Airport (PNQ), located approximately 17 km from the IISER Pune campus (~45 minutes by road, depending on traffic). It receives direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata.

By Train
Pune Junction Railway Station (PUNE) is well connected to major cities across India through frequent express and superfast trains. It is about 9 km from the campus (~30 minutes by road). The Pune Metro Aqua Line reaches Vanaz, the nearest station to campus, from where a taxi is a short ride.

By Road
Pune is well connected by several major national highways and expressways. The Mumbai–Pune Expressway links Pune to Mumbai in approximately 3 hours. Long-distance buses run from Mumbai, Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Hyderabad and Bengaluru, via MSRTC and private operators. The campus is reached from anywhere in Pune via Pashan Road.

Weather Advisory for December in Pune

December in Pune is dry and sunny, roughly 28 °C by day and 12 °C at night. Bring a light jacket for the early-morning and evening sessions.

Abstracts

There are two deadlines, and only the first is a hard cut-off:

20 September 2026 — to be considered for a short student talk. Talks are selected from the abstracts received by this date, so a late abstract cannot be considered for the podium. It falls on the same day early-bird registration closes.

20 November 2026 — for a poster. Poster abstracts are accepted right up to the registration deadline.

Submission instructions will be published here; until then, write to aicbc2026@acads.iiserpune.ac.in.
